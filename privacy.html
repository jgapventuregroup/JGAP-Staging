Privacy Policy

Staging draft for counsel review · November 3, 2026

Scope

This notice describes how The Key Ahead Investor App handles information. The legal operator, privacy contact, and effective date are pending confirmation.

Information and purposes

We process account and sign-in information to authenticate you, workspace roles to control access, and information you submit, such as property addresses, financial entries, notes, repairs, and tasks, to provide workspace tools. Browser storage supports sign-in and preferences. Authorized workspace members can access records according to their roles.

Service providers

Supabase supports sign-in and hosts app data. Stripe hosts checkout and provides billing identifiers and subscription status to the app. Address lookup sends the address being searched to a mapping service. The production mapping configuration and its data flows are pending verification.

Advertising

Live advertising is disabled in staging. The launch notice will reflect the production advertising and tracking configuration.

Retention and your choices

The final policy will identify how to contact us, how to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable, and how we determine retention for workspace data and backups.
